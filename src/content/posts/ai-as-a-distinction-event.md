title: AI as a distinction event
date: 2026-11-12
excerpt: When every brand can generate the same content, taste becomes the only moat left.
---

For a while, the hard part of brand and content work was production: writing the copy, shipping the campaign, getting the deck done on time. AI has mostly solved that part. What's left is the part that was always harder to teach — knowing which of the ten good options is the right one.

That's the distinction event. Not who can produce content, but who can tell the difference between content that's merely correct and content that's actually worth someone's attention. Teams that treat AI as a shortcut around that judgment will produce more of everything and stand out less. Teams that treat it as leverage *for* that judgment — more drafts, faster iteration, more room to be picky — will pull ahead.

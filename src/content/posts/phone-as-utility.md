title: Phone as utility
date: 2026-10-02
excerpt: Somewhere along the way the phone stopped being a tool and became the thing we reach for by default.
---

A utility is something you use when you need it and forget about otherwise — water, electricity, a dial tone. Somewhere in the last decade, the phone stopped behaving like that. It became the default reach, the thing your hand goes to in any gap of a few seconds, needed or not.

That shift matters for anyone building something people are supposed to pay attention to. You're not competing for a slot in someone's day anymore; you're competing with a reflex. The brands and products that respect that — that earn the reach instead of exploiting it — are the ones people stick with once the novelty wears off.

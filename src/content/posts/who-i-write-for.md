I don't write for "an audience." I write for a specific, slightly imagined person: a few years into their career in brand or content, probably in-house, probably at a company that's growing faster than its process can keep up with. They're asking the same questions I used to ask — how do you scale creative without losing the thing that made it good, how do you lead a team through a rebrand, how do you know when a piece of work is actually done.

If something I write doesn't help that person make a slightly better decision this week, it probably doesn't belong here.
